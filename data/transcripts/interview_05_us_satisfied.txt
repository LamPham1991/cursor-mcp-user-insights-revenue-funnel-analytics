Q: What do you care most about when sending your child to school?
A: "Ensuring that they are learning the necessary skills for their age range."

Q: How do you keep track of your child's studies?
A: "Ask my child personally if they have homework or any assignments that need
   to be done, and I stay up to date with a weekly newsletter put out by the
   school that shows the material that they are covering for the week. I'll
   even reach out to the teacher personally at times for any concerns I have."

Q: What are your biggest challenges when tracking your child's academic
   progress?
A: "I don't think I face any challenges really, one thing that helps us is to
   stay organized. We also use several different apps to help us through
   everything." [sic]

Q: You mentioned that you use other apps to help with their studies — could you
   give me an example of some you use and how they help?
A: "The Remind app allows us to get notifications whenever the teacher puts
   something out or makes an update. Infinite Campus allows me to stay up to
   date with my child's grades and allows the teacher to leave me any notes." [sic]

Q: How often do you feel the need to contact your child's teacher about their
   academic performance or behavior?
A: "Rarely ever, I don't usually have issues with that. I only really contact
   the teacher for clarification on something."

Q: How satisfied are you with the current communication tools provided by your
   child's school? (1–5, 1 = disappointed, 5 = very satisfied). Why?
A: "I'd say a 4."

Q: Why 4? What could make it a 5?
A: "I'm not sure, it's very efficient now but it's not perfect." [sic]

Q: How do you think communication between parents and teachers could be made
   more effective and convenient?
A: "I honestly don't know, I didn't have many issues with that." [sic]
